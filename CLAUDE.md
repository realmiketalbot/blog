# CLAUDE.md

Quarto blog for miketalbot.io/blog. See README.md for how posts, drafts and styling are organised.

- Before a post is published (moved from `_drafts/` into `posts/`, or edited in `posts/`), review it against the **Style guide** in README.md and flag anything that breaks it, quoting the passage and suggesting a fix. Point out problems rather than rewriting the author's voice.
- This repo is public. Never commit anything from `_drafts/`, or analysis data, unless the author explicitly asks.
- The header, footer and colour tokens mirror the main site (realmiketalbot/realmiketalbot.github.io); keep them in step when either changes.
